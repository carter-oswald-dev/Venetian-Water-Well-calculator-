How many wells would you need? Modeling drinking water the way Venice made it — one filtered sand cistern at a time

Venice had no rivers or fresh groundwater, so for over a thousand years its only water source was rain, caught on paved squares and filtered down through sand into a clay-sealed underground basin — the pozzo alla veneziana.
